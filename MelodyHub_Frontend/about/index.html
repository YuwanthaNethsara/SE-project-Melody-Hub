About MelodyHub

MelodyHub started as a simple idea: buying music gear online should feel as dependable as walking into a good record shop. We stock physical products, keep real stock counts, and show you the price you will actually pay before you check out.

How the store works

  • Every price and order total is calculated on our servers, so what you see is what you are charged.
  • Stock is reserved when you place an order and returned if the order is cancelled.
  • Products can carry a warranty. If you buy one, you can submit a claim from your account and follow its progress.
  • Artists keep their own profiles, albums and tracks up to date.

Who runs it

A small team of administrators, inventory, sales and marketing managers, and artists, each with access to only the parts of the system they need.
